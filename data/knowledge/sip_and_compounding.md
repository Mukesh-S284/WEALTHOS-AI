## What is Compound Interest?
Compound interest is the addition of interest to the principal sum of a loan or deposit, or in other words, interest on interest. Over long periods, compounding causes exponential growth of wealth. The mathematical formula for compound growth with periodic contributions is:
FV = PV * (1 + r)^n + PMT * [((1 + r)^n - 1) / r]
where PV is the present value, PMT is the regular periodic payment, r is the periodic interest rate, and n is the total number of periods.

## Systematic Investment Planning (SIP)
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is an investment vehicle offered to investors allowing them to invest small amounts periodically (weekly, monthly, quarterly) instead of lump-sum investments.
SIP enforces financial discipline through dollar-cost averaging (DCA). When prices are low, your fixed contribution buys more units; when prices are high, it buys fewer units. Over complete market cycles, this lowers the average cost per unit without requiring market timing.
Bonds provide income stability and downside protection, whereas equities provide long-term inflation-hedging capital growth.
